How it works

The local engine classifies text with TF-IDF + Logistic Regression and URLs with a Random Forest over structural features. A transparent rule-weighted risk engine turns those outputs and detected evidence into a 0-100 score. Optional AI summaries only explain the finished result.

Analyse a URL

The address is analysed as text only. It is never opened, resolved or downloaded.

Analyse a message or email

Paste the text of an SMS or email. Only structured findings are ever shared with an AI provider, never your message.
